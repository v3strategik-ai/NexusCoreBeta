# Nexus Core Brand Analysis

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors**: Dark background (#0a0a0a to #1a1a2e), Cyan/Blue glow (#00d4ff, #0099cc)
- **Logo**: Glowing brain symbol representing AI intelligence
- **Theme**: Quantum-level technology with neural network patterns
- **Style**: Futuristic, high-tech, sophisticated
- **Effects**: Particle systems, energy flows, neural connections

## Key Visual Elements
- Glowing brain icon as central logo
- Quantum energy patterns and particle effects
- Neural network connections
- Cyan/blue color palette with dark backgrounds
- Smooth animations and transitions

## Brand Personality
- Advanced and sophisticated
- Quantum-level intelligence
- Autonomous and powerful
- Professional and trustworthy
- Cutting-edge technology
